A.24 retains its squared-norm conclusion. A separate proved theorem performs
the square-root and universal-constant reparameterization before the public
theorem. The public theorem accepts arbitrary POVMs and has no bridge or
extraction premise.

The extraction API exposes a transparent pair of local isometries and its
conjugation action.  Its \texttt{SquaredRealizes} certificate preserves A.24's
squared state norm and four separate squared-family bounds (Alice/Bob, X/Z).
The robustness bridge converts it to the public \texttt{Realizes} certificate,
whose state bound is unsquared.  Nonintegral robustness powers are represented
by \texttt{Real.rpow}; they are never overloaded natural or integer powers.
