\BlueprintChapter{Finite-Field and Quantum Foundations}{foundations}

This chapter fixes finite carriers, polynomial encodings, measurement
postprocessing, state-dependent distance, and generalized Pauli algebra. The
correct trace phase replaces the false unrestricted cross-basis commutation
claim.

The Lean carrier contract is concrete.  An odd exponent $k$ selects
\texttt{GaloisField 2 k}; its field, finite, characteristic, cardinality,
finite-dimensional algebra, trace, and self-dual-basis data are derived once.
Arbitrary POVMs use the qualified \texttt{MIPStarRE.Quantum.Measurement} API,
without opening the unrelated LDT measurement hierarchy.  States live in
finite Euclidean spaces, with explicit adapters for \texttt{WithLp}, matrices,
and operator actions.  Normalization is bundled with each pure strategy.
